Textures in this pack are based on NASA elevation and imagery data.
Colors are tuned to spacecraft photography (Messenger, Viking, Cassini, Hubble).
Optional ultra maps (8K where Solar System Scope publishes them, 4K Venus
atmosphere) download from Wikimedia Commons copies of the same SSS files on
first visit and are cached in the browser. Uranus and Neptune have no map
larger than 2K on SSS.
